### 📝 Quiz 10.1: Past Simple and Past Continuous (Full Script)

| Q | Question | Options | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | We _______ (walk) in the park when it suddenly _______ (start) to rain. | A) were walking / started / B) walked / was starting / C) were walking / starting / D) walked / started | A |
| 2 | I _______ (read) a book all evening yesterday. | A) was reading / B) read / C) am reading / D) were reading | B |
| 3 | While she _______ (cook) dinner, the children _______ (do) their homework. | A) cooked / did / B) was cooking / were doing / C) is cooking / are doing / D) cooked / were doing | B |
| 4 | What _______ (you / do) when I _______ (call) you last night? | A) did you do / called / B) were you doing / called / C) did you do / was calling / D) are you doing / call | B |
| 5 | They _______ (not go) to the beach because the weather was too cold. | A) wasn't going / B) didn't go / C) weren't going / D) haven't gone | B |
| 6 | The electricity _______ (go out) while I _______ (watch) a movie. | A) went out / was watching / B) was going out / watched / C) went out / watched / D) goes out / was watching | A |
| 7 | He _______ (see) his friend at the mall two days ago. | A) was seeing / B) saw / C) sees / D) is seeing | B |
| 8 | At 8 PM last night, I _______ (study) for my English test. | A) studied / B) study / C) was studying / D) am studying | C |
| 9 | The teacher _______ (enter) the room and the students immediately _______ (stop) talking. | A) was entering / were stopping / B) entered / stopped / C) entered / were stopping / D) was entering / stopped | B |
| 10 | I _______ (sleep) when the fire alarm _______ (ring). | A) was sleeping / rang / B) slept / was ringing / C) slept / rang / D) was sleeping / was ringing | A |

### Answers:

| Q | Answer | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A | Past Continuous for the longer action (walking), Past Simple for the interruptive action (started). |
| 2 | B | Past Simple for a completed action over a specific past period (all evening yesterday). |
| 3 | B | Past Continuous in both clauses for two actions happening simultaneously (while). |
| 4 | B | Past Continuous for the background action (doing), Past Simple for the shorter action (called). |
| 5 | B | Past Simple negative for a completed action in the past. |
| 6 | A | Past Simple for the sudden interruption (went out), Past Continuous for the background action (watching). |
| 7 | B | Past Simple for a specific, completed time (two days ago). |
| 8 | C | Past Continuous for an action in progress at a specific past moment (At 8 PM last night). |
| 9 | B | Past Simple for two sequenced short actions (entered, stopped). |
| 10 | A | Past Continuous for the background action (sleeping), Past Simple for the sudden interruption (rang). |

### 📝 Quiz 10.3: Articles (A, An, The, $\emptyset$) (Full Script)

| Q | Question | Options | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | She works as _______ engineer in _______ city center. | A) an / the / B) a / the / C) the / a / D) $\emptyset$ / the | A |
| 2 | We went to _______ cinema to see _______ new movie. | A) $\emptyset$ / a / B) the / a / C) the / the / D) a / a | B |
| 3 | _______ life is getting more expensive every year. | A) The / B) An / C) A / D) $\emptyset$ | D |
| 4 | Could you hand me _______ blue pen on the desk? | A) a / B) an / C) the / D) $\emptyset$ | C |
| 5 | _______ honest man will always tell the truth. | A) A / B) An / C) The / D) $\emptyset$ | B |
| 6 | _______ President of the company gave a speech today. | A) A / B) An / C) The / D) $\emptyset$ | C |
| 7 | They are having _______ dinner at home tonight. | A) the / B) a / C) an / D) $\emptyset$ | D |
| 8 | _______ Arabian Gulf is known for its warm waters. | A) A / B) An / C) The / D) $\emptyset$ | C |
| 9 | I need _______ piece of _______ cake. | A) a / the / B) an / a / C) a / $\emptyset$ / D) $\emptyset$ / the | C |
| 10 | She usually goes to _______ work by _______ bus. | A) the / the / B) $\emptyset$ / $\emptyset$ / C) a / $\emptyset$ / D) the / a | B |

### Answers:

| Q | Answer | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A | An before a vowel sound (engineer); the for a specific location (city center). |
| 2 | B | The for common places (cinema); a for the first mention of an unspecified item (new movie). |
| 3 | D | Zero article ($\emptyset$) for general, abstract nouns (life). |
| 4 | C | The when referring to a specific, unique item visible to the listener (the blue pen). |
| 5 | B | An because the word honest starts with a vowel sound /ɒ/. |
| 6 | C | The when the title refers to a unique person in context. |
| 7 | D | Zero article ($\emptyset$) before meals when speaking generally. |
| 8 | C | The is used before the names of gulfs and seas. |
| 9 | C | A is used with piece (countable); zero article ($\emptyset$) for the general noun cake. |
| 10 | B | Zero article ($\emptyset$) for going to work (purpose) and for transport systems (by bus). |

### 📝 Quiz 12.3: Reported Speech (Questions and Commands) (Full Script)

This quiz focuses on converting direct speech questions and commands into reported speech.

| Q | Question | Options | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Direct: "Where did you go yesterday?" he asked me. Reported: He asked me _______ the day before. | A) where did I go / B) where I had gone / C) where I went / D) where had I gone | B |
| 2 | Direct: "Do you enjoy teaching?" she asked. Reported: She asked the teacher _______ teaching. | A) did she enjoy / B) if she enjoyed / C) whether did she enjoy / D) if did she enjoy | B |
| 3 | Direct: "Don't touch that button!" the supervisor ordered. Reported: The supervisor ordered the worker _______ that button. | A) not to touch / B) don't touch / C) didn't touch / D) to not touch | A |
| 4 | Direct: "Will you be ready by noon?" he questioned. Reported: He questioned if I _______ ready by noon. | A) will be / B) would be / C) am / D) had been | B |
| 5 | Direct: "Open your books to page 15," the instructor told us. Reported: The instructor told us _______ our books to page 15. | A) opening / B) open / C) to open / D) opened | C |
| 6 | Direct: "What is your name?" the policeman inquired. Reported: The policeman inquired what _______ was. | A) was my name / B) my name was / C) is my name / D) my name is | B |
| 7 | Direct: "Have you ever traveled abroad?" she wanted to know. Reported: She wanted to know _______ abroad. | A) if I have ever traveled / B) if I had ever traveled / C) if I traveled ever / D) had I ever traveled | B |
| 8 | Direct: "Please wait for me," the girl begged. Reported: The girl begged her friend _______ her. | A) to wait for / B) wait for / C) waiting for / D) waits for | A |
| 9 | Direct: "Why is the door locked?" he asked them. Reported: He asked them why the door _______. | A) was locked / B) is locked / C) had been locked / D) has been locked | A |
| 10 | Direct: "Where do you live?" the interviewer asked. Reported: The interviewer asked me where I _______. | A) live / B) lived / C) did live / D) was living | B |

### Answers:

| Q | Answer | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | B | Wh-question + S + V. Past Simple (did go) shifts to Past Perfect (had gone). Yesterday shifts to the day before. |
| 2 | B | Yes/No question uses if/whether + S + V. Simple Present shifts to Simple Past. |
| 3 | A | Negative command uses not + to + V1 (Infinitive). |
| 4 | B | Will shifts to would. |
| 5 | C | Positive command uses to + V1 (Infinitive). |
| 6 | B | The structure must be S + V (indirect speech order: my name was). Simple Present shifts to Simple Past. |
| 7 | B | Present Perfect shifts to Past Perfect. |
| 8 | A | Positive command/request uses to + V1. |
| 9 | A | Simple Present passive shifts to Simple Past passive (is locked $\rightarrow$ was locked). |
| 10 | B | Wh-question + S + V. Simple Present shifts to Simple Past. |

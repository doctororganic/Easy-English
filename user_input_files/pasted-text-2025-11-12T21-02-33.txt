3. Priority 3 – Vocabulary Unit Switching

* Keep the unit list in a central store (even a plain object).
* On grade change, reset both currentUnit and currentExample.
* Use DevTools → Components (React DevTools extension, free) to confirm the state updates cascade through the tree.

4. Priority 4 – Writing Section (Empty Shell)
Goal: Deliver at least one functioning writing exercise quickly.
